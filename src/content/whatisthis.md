# What is this?
> "If you want to master something, teach it."

\- Richard Feynman

---
I have found the above quote to become truer each year at university. Teaching topics to ambushed housemates or inanimate objects has been how I have taught myself the university course. I have also learnt I learn best through interactive tools and toys that give me immediate feedback. This project combines the best of both worlds, giving me an interactive tool to learn and explore Modal Logic and Kripke Semantics in depth while also giving me an outlet for my desire to teach. There is no guarantee these lessons are of any use to others but they have helped me achieve my goal of understanding.

I got to Kripke Semantics as my desired topic of self study through being introduced to similar topics that have captured my interest over the years:
- [Linguistics]{Thank you Tom Scott}
- Labled transition systems
- Graph Theory
- Linear Temporal Logic
- [Formal Specification and Verification]{Doing this for my dissertation!}
- Buchii automata
